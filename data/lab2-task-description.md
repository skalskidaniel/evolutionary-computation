Greedy regret heuristics
Implement two methods based on both the extended version of the nearest neighbor heuristic and
the greedy cycle heuristic:
• Greedy 2-regret heuristics.
• Greedy heuristics with a weighted sum criterion – 2-regret + best change of the objective
function. By default use equal weights but you can also experiment with other values.
The structure of the report should be the same as previously. In the summary of results please
include results of the previous methods.
